# Glass trophies implementation

The Founder approved implementation of the glass room, rather than another mockup.

Integrated review: PM and strategy approve ten fixed room families while retaining the complete collection. UX requires clear glass with sage edges and physical shelf contact. Learning and behavioral review require recognizable, structurally distinct stages without implying unearned mastery. Architecture requires a shared renderer, explicit stable-ID atlas regions and a load-error fallback. Analytics requires unchanged events, unlock conditions, XP and measured time. QA requires all forty real stages, transparency, desktop/mobile geometry, keyboard access and observable runtime verification.

Scope: 8/6/3/3/4/4/3/3/1/5 existing stages; fixed four upper trophies, five lower trophies and the study clock on the table. Other families remain available in the collection. No catalog or progress migration.

Assets use the built-in imagegen tool and the approved two glass progression sheets as material references. Production atlases contain isolated transparent trophies, no labels or room backdrop. Prompts describe exact real stage counts, clear glass, polished green edges, warm daylight, ivory pedestals and consistent baselines. Source outputs are preserved under the Codex generated-images directory; project assets live in `apps/web/public/achievements/glass/`.

Asset prompt set (built-in imagegen, transparent background):

- Practice atlas: regular 8×5 grid; eight progressively layered hexagonal checks, six increasingly open-page books, three paired repair-arrow sculptures, three flasks, four ringed data cylinders. Unoccupied cells remain transparent. Colorless glass, polished sage edges, white highlights, ivory pedestals; no text, shelves, room or opaque fill.
- Rhythm atlas: regular 5×5 grid; four return loops, three smooth wave sculptures, three four-pane arrangements, one fully developed ascending analysis chart and five increasingly fluid melting clocks. Unoccupied cells remain transparent. Same material and light, generous margins and aligned pedestal baselines.
- Stage-three refinement: two isolated trophies side by side; left check with three clearly separated nested hexagonal frames, right book with three clearly separated page pairs. Same clear-glass material and ivory bases. Generated separately after independent QA found the original second and third stages insufficiently distinct.
- Stage-six refinement: one hexagonal check surrounded by two separate glass orbital loops, one horizontal and one upright. Same clear-glass material and ivory base. This replaces the sixth task milestone's originally too-similar single ribbon.
